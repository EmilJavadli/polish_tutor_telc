# 9-month TELC Polski B1 plan (1 hour a day)

39 weeks x 7 days = 273 lessons. Days 1-6: new lesson; day 7: weekly review.
Every lesson trains reading, listening, vocabulary, grammar (Elementy języka), writing and speaking.

## Daily situations

**Week 1: Introductions**: greeting a neighbour in the stairwell; introducing yourself to a new colleague; ordering coffee in a café; spelling your name at a reception desk; exchanging phone numbers and e-mails; saying where you come from

**Week 2: Family and people**: talking about your family; describing a friend; looking at a family photo; a child's day at the nursery; visiting grandparents; the neighbours' family

**Week 3: Home and flat**: my flat in Kraków; rooms and furniture; viewing a flat to rent; no hot water in the flat; moving in; what is in the kitchen

**Week 4: Food and shopping**: at the bakery; at the market; in the supermarket; prices and paying; a shopping list; cooking dinner at home

**Week 5: Work and professions**: my job; a day at the office; colleagues and the boss; different professions; lunch break at work; working from home

**Week 6: Daily routine and time**: my morning; what time is it?; plans for the week; the weekend; appointments in the calendar; an evening at home

**Week 7: City and transport**: buying a tram ticket; asking for directions; taking a taxi; at the railway station; rush hour; cycling in the city

**Week 8: Free time (A1 review)**: hobbies; sport; going to the cinema; meeting friends; a walk in the park; a nice weekend

**Week 9: What happened? (past events)**: last weekend; a trip to Zakopane; a birthday party; a visit to the doctor last week; what I did yesterday; a lost wallet

**Week 10: Health**: at the family doctor; at the pharmacy; booking an appointment by phone; at the dentist; a healthy lifestyle; sick leave (L4) and calling work

**Week 11: Plans and the future**: holiday plans; weekend plans; New Year's resolutions; planning a child's birthday; learning plans; career plans

**Week 12: People and relationships**: describing appearance; describing character; new colleagues; neighbours; friends from abroad; a wedding invitation

**Week 13: Shopping and services**: clothes and sizes; returning a product; online shopping and delivery; post office and parcel lockers; at the hairdresser; shoe repair and dry cleaning

**Week 14: Eating out and cooking**: booking a table; ordering in a restaurant; Polish cuisine; a recipe; a food delivery app; dietary preferences

**Week 15: Home life and repairs**: a plumber's visit; a housing community meeting; paying the bills; the building administrator; sorting rubbish; noisy neighbours

**Week 16: Travel**: booking a hotel; at the hotel reception; a train journey to Gdańsk; at the airport; renting a car; holidays by the Baltic Sea

**Week 17: Formal situations**: at the bank; opening a bank account; at a municipal office; registering your address; phoning an institution; filling in a form

**Week 18: Looking for a job**: job advertisements; writing a CV; a job interview; phoning about a job offer; types of work contracts; the first day at a new job

**Week 19: At work**: a team meeting; an e-mail to a colleague; a conflict at work; asking for a day off; a business trip; work-life balance

**Week 20: Education and courses**: a language course; signing up for a course; studying in Poland; online learning; kindergarten and school; learning as an adult

**Week 21: Housing in detail**: renting vs buying; a rental agreement; complaining to the landlord; a renovation; searching for a flat online; moving out and the deposit

**Week 22: Health and lifestyle**: healthy eating; a fitness club; stress; sleep; public vs private healthcare; at the hospital

**Week 23: Media and technology**: smartphones; social media; online news; problems with the internet provider; online privacy; technology at work

**Week 24: Consumer life and complaints**: a complaint about a product; a delayed delivery; bad service; a warranty; a customer service call; consumer rights

**Week 25: Environment and the city**: smog in Kraków; public transport vs the car; green spaces in the city; recycling; climate and weather; a local city initiative

**Week 26: Culture and traditions**: Christmas Eve and Easter; name days; a concert; a museum visit; Polish cinema; a book I read

**Week 27: Free time and travel**: hiking in the mountains; travelling with a child; a package tour vs travelling alone; camping; a city break; problems when travelling

**Week 28: Society and opinions**: living abroad; volunteering; generations; family models; working parents; foreigners in Poland

**Week 29: Money and finances**: a household budget; a bank loan; saving money; taxes; prices and inflation; insurance

**Week 30: Everyday problems**: a lost document; a car breakdown and the mechanic; a misunderstanding; asking for help; an accident at home; the emergency number 112

**Week 31: Offices and administration**: a residence card appointment; collecting documents; at the voivodeship office; changing your address; sworn translation of documents; social insurance and benefits

**Week 32: Children and family life**: kindergarten enrolment; a sick child; at the playground; parental leave; a family weekend; a children's party

**Week 33: Career and development**: a promotion; a training course; a presentation at work; remote vs office work; negotiating; retirement

**Week 34: Services and appointments**: a car service; a phone repair; booking a beautician; a dance course for adults; a gym membership; cancelling a contract

**Week 35: Current issues**: car-free city centres; working hours; children's screen time; tourism in Kraków; healthy food in schools; Sunday shopping ban

**Week 36: TELC writing: semi-formal e-mails**: asking about a course; a complaint to a hotel; changing a booking; a request to the landlord; asking a school for information; applying for voluntary work

**Week 37: TELC speaking: experiences, presentations, discussions**: talking about a past experience; presenting a topic; describing pictures; giving your opinion; pros and cons; answering follow-up questions

**Week 38: TELC listening and reading strategies**: voice messages; everyday dialogues; a radio interview; internet forum posts; advertisements and notices; official information

**Week 39: Final review**: everyday life; work; health; travel; services; society
